# Watt Watch: Appliance Energy Consumption Website

Exercise T01(a). A three page website with placeholder content about appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

## Pages

- Home
- Televisions
- About Us

Navigation between pages is done with JavaScript (`script.js`). The logo in the top left returns to the Home page, nav links highlight on mouse over, and the current page is highlighted.

## Data Source:
    The placeholder content and data referenced in this project are inspired by publicly available datasets, including the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) Household Energy Consumption Survey and the Australian Government's Energy Rating website. For the purpose of this academic exercise, mock data representing typical Australian household appliance usage has been generated.

## Data Processing:
    The raw data (simulated for this prototype) is structured to represent average wattage and estimated daily usage hours for common household appliances, specifically focusing on televisions. The data is processed directly within the front-end JavaScript to calculate estimated daily and annual energy consumption (kWh) and associated costs (based on a mock average Australian electricity tariff of $0.30/kWh).
    
## Privacy: 
    This website does not collect, store, or process any personal user data. There are no cookies, tracking scripts, or forms that capture personally identifiable information (PII). All data visualisations are based on aggregated, publicly available, or simulated datasets.
    
## Accuracy and Limitations:
Accuracy:The data presented is for demonstration purposes only. Actual appliance energy consumption varies significantly based on the specific make and model, age of the appliance, usage patterns (e.g., brightness settings, standby time), and local climate conditions.

## Limitations:
The visualisations do not account for complex variables such as time-of-use electricity tariffs, solar panel generation, or the specific energy efficiency star ratings of individual models. The dataset is a simplified representation and should not be used as a sole source for financial or purchasing decisions.

## Ethics:
    This project adheres to ethical data visualisation practices by ensuring transparency regarding the simulated nature of the data. The visualisations are designed to inform and empower users, not to mislead or cause alarm. There is no manipulation of scales or misleading colour choices that could distort the user's understanding of energy consumption.
